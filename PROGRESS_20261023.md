## REAL data: M1600 temporal prediction ablation
Training scan indices = [15,20,25]; independent check = [30,35,40].
For each angular bin present in ALL six frames, compare a single training
scan to the median range of the check scans, versus the median of three
training frames to the same check scans.

| Angular bin width | Common cells | Raw single frame abs range disagreement P95 | Three-frame temporal median P95 | Raw P99 | Median P99 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.05° | 4,046 | 101.5 mm | 24.0 mm | 779.2 mm | 542.2 mm |
| 0.10° | 6,591 | 30.7 mm | 12.0 mm | 381.8 mm | 119.7 mm |
| 0.20° | 7,415 | 24.0 mm | 10.4 mm | 349.8 mm | 67.5 mm |

At 0.1° raw median = 2.667 mm vs three-frame median 2.0 mm, and raw
P95 = 30.667 mm vs temporal median 12.0 mm.

**Interpretation:** promising reduction in *cross-frame range disagreement*,
not a demonstrated improvement in physical wall flatness, reconstruction
resolution, or registered map accuracy. Coarser bins alter correspondence
and mix edges. No optical/LiDAR alignment or world ground truth. Large
99th-percentile differences reveal depth boundaries/occlusions or other
unresolved effects; indiscriminate smoothing/reprojection would be misleading.

## Edge-risk ablation, same 0.1-degree six-frame dataset (additional REAL result)
The first training frame alone marks a candidate boundary when adjacent
angular range bins in the SAME laser channel differ by >=100 mm.
This is a heuristic flag, NOT a validated real-edge label or rejection rule.

- 243 flagged matched cells: raw single-frame vs held-out P50 = 118.7 mm,
  P95 = 955.2 mm. Three-frame temporal median vs held-out P50 = 6.0 mm,
  P95 = 662.0 mm. The large error tail persists.
- 6,348 unflagged matched cells: raw vs held-out P50 = 2.667 mm,
  P95 = 20.0 mm. Temporal median P50 = 1.6 mm, P95 = 10.0 mm.
- The flag is computed using the first training frame, which is also the
  raw baseline. The flagged error figures are therefore selected by the
  baseline sample itself, not an unbiased true-boundary validation.
- Keep original measured returns. Mark suspected boundaries/occlusions as
  uncertain until independently corroborated. Do not smooth or delete
  risky points on the live sensor.
